Questions?

Frequently Asked

I've never done yoga before. Can I still join?

+

Absolutely. Most of our students start as complete beginners. Mohanapriya guides you step-by-step, with modifications for every pose. You'll feel comfortable from your very first session.

What do I need for an online class?

+

Just a yoga mat (or a thick blanket), comfortable clothes, and a device with a camera so Mohanapriya can guide you with real-time corrections. That's it.

What makes SVYASA training different?

+

SVYASA (Swami Vivekananda Yoga Anusandhana Samsthana) is a deemed-to-be university dedicated to yoga research. Their training integrates scientific evidence with classical yoga, ensuring practices are both authentic and therapeutically effective.

Can yoga help with my back pain or stress?

+

Yes. Research from institutions like SVYASA shows regular yoga practice significantly reduces chronic back pain and lowers cortisol (the stress hormone). Mohanapriya's classes specifically address these common issues with targeted therapeutic techniques.

How do I book a class?

+

Simply tap the WhatsApp button and send a message. Mohanapriya will personally respond, understand your needs, and schedule your free trial class. It takes less than a minute.
